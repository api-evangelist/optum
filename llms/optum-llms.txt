# Optum

> Optum, part of UnitedHealth Group, operates one of the largest U.S. medical and pharmacy networks and publishes RESTful JSON/X12 EDI and FHIR healthcare APIs for eligibility, claims, claim status, ERA/remittance, prior authorization, attachments, dental, pharmacy, and patient-access interoperability. APIs use OAuth2 client-credentials bearer tokens over TLS 1.2+, with free sandbox access and downloadable OpenAPI specs.

## APIs

- [Eligibility and Claims (Medical Network)](https://developer.optum.com/eligibilityandclaims): eligibility/v3, professional & institutional claims, claim status, ERA reports, prior authorization, attachments, payer list. Base: https://apigw.optum.com/medicalnetwork/
- [Data Access and Interoperability (FHIR R4)](https://developer.optum.com/dataaccessandinteroperability): Patient Access FHIR resources — Endpoint, Procedure, DocumentReference, ObservationVitalSigns, Immunization.
- [Optum Real for Dental](https://developer.optum.com/optumreal-dental): dental pre-care estimates, eligibility, claims, attachments, ERA.

## Authentication

- [Security and Authorization V2](https://developer.optum.com/apitools/reference/security-and-authorization-v2-overview): OAuth2 client-credentials grant; POST client_id/client_secret to the token endpoint for a 1-hour JWT bearer token.
- Token URL (production): https://apigw.optum.com/apip/auth/v2/token
- Token URL (sandbox): https://sandbox-apigw.optum.com/apip/auth/v2/token
